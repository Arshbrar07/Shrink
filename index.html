mechanism

How the loop runs

Three steps, repeating on a fixed interval — the same cycle, indefinitely. 70% of supply is committed to permanent burn, and the loop doesn't stop there.

01 / SEND

Tokens enter the pool

Holders or treasury route $SHRINK to the compaction wallet — a public address anyone can watch.

02 / COMPACT

A fixed share is burned

A set percentage of the pool's balance is sent to a dead address on a fixed interval. No discretion involved.

03 / REPEAT

The remainder resets

What's left waits for the next cycle — smaller each time, curving toward zero without ever reaching it.

verification

Public burn wallet

faq

Questions

How much of the supply gets burned?+
70% of total supply is committed to permanent burn. That's not a one-time event and it's not a ceiling — once the 70% mark is crossed, the compaction loop keeps running on the same fixed interval, burning further into the remaining supply.
Does supply ever actually hit zero?+
No. Each cycle burns a fraction of whatever remains, so the curve approaches zero asymptotically. In practice supply becomes negligible long before it reaches true zero.
What's the burn rate?+
A fixed percentage of the pool's holdings, burned on a set interval. The rate is published in the docs and locked into the contract after launch — it isn't adjustable.
Who controls the bot?+
The bot only executes the burn function. There's no admin key that can pause the loop, redirect tokens, or mint new supply.
Can I add tokens to the loop myself?+
Yes — send $SHRINK to the compaction wallet and it gets burned on the next cycle. It doesn't have to come from treasury.
